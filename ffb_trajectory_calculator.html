Engineering note: These are idealised estimates. The solid-sphere rolling model assumes no slipping and ignores the irregular shape, deformation, rolling resistance, tumbling and impacts. Do not use this alone to size the chute or bucket supports.
Model: g = 9.81 m/s². After leaving the plane, the FFB is treated as a projectile with no air resistance. Bucket horizontal position is measured from the plane start and stays fixed as angle changes; rim height and opening width are adjustable. The calculator checks a simplified rim-crossing condition; detailed collisions and FFB tumbling are not simulated.
